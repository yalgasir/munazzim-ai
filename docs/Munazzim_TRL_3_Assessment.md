# Munazzim AI Time Manager: TRL 3 Assessment

**Assessment date:** 2026-09-03
**Final assessment:** **PASS**

## TRL Level and Scope

TRL 3 establishes analytical and experimental proof of concept. For this research prototype, the relevant question is whether the proposed AI-assisted scheduling, structured-output, and persistence concepts can execute as an implemented prototype; it is not a production qualification.

## Evidence Considered

- Implemented task and appointment routes, AI Assistant workflow, and AI Performance workflow.
- A central Qwen-first/Ollama-fallback AI gateway with structured-output support.
- Local validation and sanitization that reject malformed or invalid proposed schedule data.
- Documented functional exercises of AI Assistant, AI Performance, task/appointment persistence, and validation behavior.

## Assessment

The critical concepts have progressed beyond design: an integrated implementation accepts scheduling input, obtains structured AI output, validates it locally, and supports user-confirmed task and appointment actions. The evidence is appropriate to proof of concept.

## Boundaries

This result does not verify primary-Qwen application integration, production authentication, production persistence, semantic correctness across representative data, or production reliability.

## Final Assessment

**PASS.** Munazzim demonstrates proof of concept for its principal AI-assisted scheduling and data-handling functions.
