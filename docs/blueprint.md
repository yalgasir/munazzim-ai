# **App Name**: منظّم

## Core Features:

- User Authentication: Secure user registration, login, and profile management leveraging Firebase Authentication to protect personal data.
- Dashboard & Calendar Views: A comprehensive dashboard providing an overview of daily tasks and appointments, alongside integrated daily, weekly, and monthly calendar views with full Arabic RTL support.
- Appointment Management: Tools for adding, editing, deleting, and searching appointments with flexible scheduling options and recurring event support.
- Task Management: Organize tasks with customizable priorities (High, Medium, Low), track completion, and set due dates for effective workflow management.
- Smart Reminders & Notifications: Configurable, timely alerts for upcoming appointments, task deadlines, and important events.
- AI Time Management Assistant: An AI-powered tool utilizing qwen/qwen3-coder:free via OpenRouter to analyze the user's schedule, task load, and historical productivity, offering personalized suggestions for optimal time allocation and schedule adjustments.
- Productivity Statistics: A dedicated section presenting visual analytics and reports on completed tasks, time distribution, and productivity trends over customizable periods.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A deep, professional sapphire blue (#2963CC), symbolizing trust, efficiency, and focus. This choice grounds the modern aesthetic with a sense of calm authority.
- Background color: A very light, desaturated blue-grey (#ECF1F8) to maintain clarity and offer a spacious feel, designed for readability and visual comfort in the light mode.
- Accent color: A vibrant turquoise-blue (#52B2BF), chosen for its invigorating contrast to the primary blue. It will highlight interactive elements and important information, subtly suggesting growth and new insights.
- Headline and body font (Latin): 'Inter' (sans-serif), selected for its modern, clear, and objective aesthetic, ensuring legibility and a contemporary feel across the interface. An appropriate Arabic font will be used for all RTL content to match the modern and professional look.
- Utilize minimalist, line-art icons that clearly convey meaning and integrate seamlessly with both light and dark modes, ensuring global recognition and clarity within the Arabic RTL interface.
- A clean, card-based responsive design with meticulous attention to Arabic RTL conventions for text flow and component alignment. Generous spacing and clear hierarchy will improve readability and user experience on all devices.
- Subtle and purposeful animations will enhance user feedback during task completion, calendar view transitions, and notification alerts, providing a fluid and intuitive interaction without distraction.
